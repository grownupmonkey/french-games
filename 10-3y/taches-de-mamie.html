Mamie Jeanne :
Pour le prof : how this scene works
  • Requests arrive in four forms, chosen at random: Tu dois laver la vaisselle, Lave la vaisselle, s’il te plaît, Tu peux laver la vaisselle ? and Il faut laver la vaisselle. Students connect each command form (including mets, fais, sors, essuie, promène) to the job.
  • Students answer “Qu’est-ce que tu fais ?” in the je form (je lave, je mets, je fais, je sors, j’essuie, je promène), so they conjugate in their heads. Each object also offers one tu-form distractor (tu laves la vaisselle), which gets a targeted correction.
  • Several objects have more than one real job (laver / essuyer la vaisselle, laver / plier le linge, mettre / débarrasser la table, promener le chien / donner à manger au chien), so the verb matters, not just the noun.
  • Twelve jobs win the round. Each expired request costs a heart; losing three hearts ends the round early. Mode calme removes the clock. Défi : audio seulement hides request text until Voir le texte, which is counted.
  • The round ends with a short note: one sentence with aller + infinitive (Demain, je vais…) and one with devoir + infinitive (Samedi, je dois…). A conjugated verb after vais or dois (je vais range) gets a targeted correction.
  • Outcomes practised: 10 A 4 (directive function), 10 R 2.5d and 2.5f (present tense, regular and irregular), 10 R 2.5h (devoir, pouvoir + infinitive), 10 R 2.6b (le futur proche), 10 R 2.8a (commands). Household responsibilities are a 30-3Y context (30 C 1.3), used here with 10-3Y language.
